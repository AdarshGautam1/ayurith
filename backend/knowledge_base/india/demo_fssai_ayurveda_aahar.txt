FSSAI Ayurveda Aahar Regulations
"Ayurveda Aahar" means a food prepared in accordance with the recipes or formulation and/or processes described in the authoritative books of Ayurveda listed under ‘Schedule A’ of these regulations.
It does not include Ayurvedic drugs or proprietary Ayurvedic medicines. It does not include medicines for treating or curing diseases.
Only permitted additives and ingredients specified by FSSAI can be used. Claims on packaging must be strictly in accordance with classical texts and must not claim to cure or mitigate any specific disease.
